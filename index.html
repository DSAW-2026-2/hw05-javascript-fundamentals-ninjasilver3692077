PlayReal: group challenges people actually complete.

A web application for small student groups to create challenges, upload evidence, vote, earn points, maintain streaks, and compete in a shared ranking.

The Problem

Challenges organized through chats quickly become difficult to follow

Evidence, votes, points, and progress become mixed with regular conversations, making group challenges difficult to organize over time.

Evidence

Photos and comments become mixed with normal group conversations.

Scoring

Votes and points often require someone to track everything manually.

Progress

Rankings, streaks, and challenge history are difficult to maintain.

Web Application Justification

Why the tools students already use are not enough

PlayReal needs collaborative participation, evidence submission, voting, points, streaks, rankings, and frequent updates from several members.

Messaging Apps

Useful for communication, but challenge content becomes mixed with ordinary conversations.

Spreadsheets

They can store scores, but require manual updates and are not focused on participation.

PlayReal

Challenges, evidence, voting, scores, streaks, and rankings stay organized in one place.

The Solution

One clear flow for every group challenge

Every challenge follows the same simple process from joining a group to viewing the final ranking.

  1. 01

    Group

    Create or join a private group with friends.

  2. 02

    Challenge

    View the active challenge and its deadline.

  3. 03

    Evidence

    Upload evidence and a short comment.

  4. 04

    Voting

    Review submissions and vote with the group.

  5. 05

    Scoring

    Earn points and maintain challenge streaks.

  6. 06

    Ranking

    Compare progress with the rest of the group.

Target Users

Designed for small private student groups

PlayReal is designed for university students, classmates, and small groups of friends who want to stay motivated through shared challenges.

Habit Challenges

Reading, studying, exercise, hydration, language practice, and other recurring habits.

Friendly Competitions

Group activities supported by evidence, voting, points, streaks, and rankings.

User Stories

Core PlayReal interactions

These stories describe the main actions students complete while participating in PlayReal.

Story 01

Create a Private Group

As a user, I want to create a private group so that I can participate in challenges with my friends.

Story 02

View the Active Challenge

As a participant, I want to see the active challenge so that I know what I need to complete.

Story 03

Upload Evidence

As a participant, I want to upload evidence so that my group can see that I completed the challenge.

Story 04

Vote for Evidence

As a participant, I want to vote for a submission so that I can help select the best participation.

Story 05

Earn Points

As a participant, I want to earn points so that my progress is recorded.

Story 06

View the Ranking

As a participant, I want to view the ranking so that I can compare my progress with the group.
